# Sona Community Code of Conduct

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

Sona welcomes people of every background and experience level. Contributors
must communicate respectfully, discuss ideas rather than attack people, accept
constructive feedback, and protect other people's privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, sexualized attention, publishing private information, or repeatedly
ignoring a maintainer's reasonable moderation request.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily
limit participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect
the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented, and
made in good faith.

For a private report, use the repository Security tab's **Report a
vulnerability** form and state that the report concerns community conduct.

---

## 简体中文

Sona 欢迎任何背景和经验水平的参与者。贡献者应保持尊重，讨论观点而不是攻击
个人，接受建设性反馈，并保护他人隐私。

不可接受的行为包括骚扰、歧视、威胁、故意扰乱讨论、性化关注、公开他人隐私，
或反复无视维护者合理的管理要求。

为保护社区，维护者可以编辑或删除评论、拒绝贡献、暂时限制参与或永久禁止参与。
处理措施应与问题程度相称、有记录，并基于善意判断。

如需私密报告，请使用仓库 Security 页面中的 **Report a vulnerability**，并注明
这是社区行为问题。
